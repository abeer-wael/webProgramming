Stanford: 4 on-campus beverages you should
be drinking

1.

Italian soda at Bytes

350 Serra Mall, Stanford, CA

When's the last time you've had an Italian soda? It had been a while for me, but I wanted to join my friend for a non-caffeinated beverage at Bytes and I became reacquainted with the combination of fizzy water and artificial fruit syrup. Italian sodas are not so sweet as a normal soda and not so boring as a flavored carbonated water.

Recommendation: Raspberry Italian soda
